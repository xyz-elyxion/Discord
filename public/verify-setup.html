🍋 Interactive Verification for your server

Give your members the same smooth verification Limey V1 uses: a native in-client card for mod users, a human challenge for everyone else, and a read-only verification channel. You need Manage Server permission in the server and the Limey bot in it.

Checking your session…

Configure

Pick the channel where the verification card lives and the role members receive after verifying. The bot will make the channel read-only and keep the card pinned.

How it works for your members

  1. The bot posts a verification card in your chosen channel and keeps it read-only.
  2. Limey V1 users get a native in-client card (the InteractiveVerification plugin ships with the mod).
  3. Everyone else uses the website: Discord login → 3-round human challenge → verified.
  4. Verified members get your role — done.
