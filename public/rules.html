Official community guidelines

Rules of Limey V1

A professional and welcoming environment for users, developers, contributors, and enthusiasts. By participating in this community, you agree to follow these guidelines.

1 Respect & Professional Conduct

All members are expected to communicate respectfully and professionally. Constructive criticism and disagreements are welcome when expressed respectfully.

  • No harassment, bullying, intimidation, or personal attacks.
  • No discrimination based on race, nationality, gender, religion, disability, or any other protected characteristic.
  • No excessive hostility, unnecessary arguments, or attempts to provoke conflict.
  • No targeting individuals or groups with malicious intent.

2 Community Integrity

Members must contribute positively to the community. Do not:

  • Intentionally spread misinformation.
  • Impersonate developers, staff members, or other users.
  • Attempt to manipulate, deceive, or mislead community members.
  • Create unnecessary drama or conflicts.

3 Security & Safety

Protecting users is a priority. The following are strictly prohibited:

  • Malware, viruses, token stealers, credential theft tools, or malicious code.
  • Sharing suspicious files, links, or downloads.
  • Requesting passwords, authentication tokens, private keys, or personal information.
  • Attempts to compromise accounts, systems, or services.

Security issues should be reported privately to the moderation or development team.

4 Plugins, Modifications & Development

This community supports development, customization, and experimentation — but projects and contributions must not:

  • Enable abuse, spam, harassment, or malicious automation.
  • Facilitate account compromise or unauthorized access.
  • Violate Discord's Terms of Service.
  • Include hidden functionality or undisclosed tracking.

Developers are encouraged to provide clear documentation, credit third-party work appropriately, follow open-source licensing requirements, and submit responsible bug reports and feature requests.

5 Content Guidelines

All shared content must be appropriate for a general audience. Prohibited content includes:

  • NSFW or sexually explicit material.
  • Graphic violence or disturbing content.
  • Illegal content or instructions facilitating illegal activity.
  • Hate speech or extremist material.
  • Excessive profanity or inappropriate content.

6 Advertising & Promotion

To maintain a focused community, do not:

  • Advertise other servers, products, services, or websites without permission.
  • Send unsolicited advertisements through direct messages.
  • Promote unrelated projects in support channels.

Partnerships and collaborations must be approved by staff.

7 Channel Usage

Please use channels for their intended purpose:

  • Read channel descriptions before posting.
  • Keep discussions relevant.
  • Avoid posting support requests in unrelated channels.
  • Avoid excessive mentions or unnecessary notifications.

8 Support Guidelines

When requesting help, please provide:

  • A clear description of the issue.
  • Relevant error messages or logs.
  • Steps to reproduce the problem.
  • System information when necessary.

Please remain patient while waiting for assistance. Community members and staff volunteer their time to help.

9 Bug Reports & Feedback

Quality feedback helps improve the project. When reporting issues:

  • Confirm the issue exists before submitting reports.
  • Avoid duplicate reports.
  • Provide accurate information.
  • Do not intentionally submit false reports.

Feature suggestions are welcome but may not always be implemented.

10 Privacy & Personal Information

Respect the privacy of others. Never share personal information, private conversations without consent, account credentials, or private files or data belonging to others. Do not attempt to collect personal information from community members.

11 Moderation Policy

Moderators reserve the right to take action against behaviour that negatively impacts the community. Possible actions include warnings, message removal, temporary restrictions, temporary bans, and permanent bans.

Moderation decisions are based on context, severity, and previous behaviour. If you believe a moderation action was incorrect, contact staff privately through the appropriate channels.

12 Discord Terms of Service

All members must follow the Discord Terms of Service, the Discord Community Guidelines, and applicable laws and regulations. Breaking Discord's rules may result in removal from the community.

Thank you

Our goal is to maintain a high-quality, safe, and collaborative environment for everyone.
Thank you for helping make Limey V1 a professional community for developers, contributors, and users.
