Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

What we store

We keep data collection minimal. Depending on the features you use, we may store:

  • Discord user ID — to identify you for verification, Limes wallet, XP/levels, counting stats and linked roles.
  • Username / avatar — shown in leaderboards and detector listings.
  • Verification + moderation records — tokens, verified status, warnings.
  • Limes wallet data — balance, streaks, transactions, redeemed codes.
  • Server configs — settings chosen by admins (welcomer, counting, leveling, verification channels/roles) for servers the bot is in.
  • Settings sync — if you enable cloud sync, your Limey V1 settings are stored so they can be restored on other devices.

We do not store your Discord token, password, email, or message contents. Counting and XP track aggregates, not chat logs.

Why we store it

Purely to run the Services: granting verified roles, keeping your wallet and levels, syncing your settings, and letting server admins configure the bot. We don't sell or share your data with third parties, and we don't use it for advertising.

Third parties

The Services run on hosting providers (e.g. Render for the website and bot, and a managed PostgreSQL provider for persistent storage). Discord itself handles all Discord OAuth logins — we never see your Discord password.

Retention & deletion

Wallet, level and config data is kept while the Services operate. You can request deletion of your stored data (or delete your synced settings from the dashboard) — join the community server and ask a maintainer, or use the contacts on the dashboard. Deleting data removes your verification status, balance and levels.

Questions

Questions about this policy? Reach the maintainers via the community server.
